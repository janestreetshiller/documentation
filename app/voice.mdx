<h2 id="voice">Voice Wake requirements</h2>

<p>Voice Wake requires explicit opt-in, microphone access, speech recognition authorization and on-device recognition availability. Microphone permission alone does not establish that wake listening is available.</p><p>Recording, dictation, secure input or a native dialog can suspend listening. Finish the active task, then try again. If Voice Wake still fails, use the <a href="/app/troubleshooting#recover-voice">Voice Wake checks</a> and record the visible error with your app and macOS versions.</p><p>The macOS microphone privacy indicator is OS-owned. Leave it visible while listening is active.</p>
