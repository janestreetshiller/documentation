<h2 id="faq-download">Can I download Ouros?</h2>

<p>Yes. Ouros Bar 1.0 downloads free from your signed-in account. See <a href="/app/install">Install and compatibility</a> for the macOS and hardware requirements.</p>

<h2 id="faq-helper">Does the recovery helper run my command?</h2>

<p>No. The <a href="https://ouros.md/docs/troubleshooting#recovery">recovery helper</a> previews documented leading-symbol rules and filters recovery checks. Your optional attempt stays in that page, is not sent or saved, and clears on reload.</p>

<h2 id="faq-products">How do the app, CLI and Loop Builder relate?</h2>

<p>The macOS Ouros Bar and Loop Builder have separate account and service requirements. The current <a href="https://ouros.md/cli">CLI product page</a> marks RX unavailable. See <a href="/loop-builder">Loop Builder</a> for its workspace checks.</p>

<h2 id="faq-billing">Where are billing details?</h2>

<p>See <a href="https://ouros.md/pricing">Pricing</a> and <a href="/credits">Credits and billing</a>. These docs do not grant access or establish that a Gateway run is included in your account.</p>
